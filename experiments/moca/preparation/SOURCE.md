## Materials deliberately excluded

No LICENSE/COPYING or dataset redistribution grant was identified in the pinned official repository. Public availability is not treated as permission to redistribute its stories, source questions, individual votes or original code. These materials are not bundled. Only model choices/probabilities, aggregate findings and independently written preparation/analysis code are included. The paper has its own publication terms; they are not assumed to license the dataset.

The local preparation command requires causal_dataset_v1.json and moral_dataset_v1.json. Their pinned SHA-256 values are checked in preparation/code/prepare_data.py. Raw sources must stay outside the checkout; generated data/dataset.json is ignored and must be removed before distributing a directory archive.

The preparation script makes no download or model call. Access to the original source is needed for input-level reconstruction; excluded source material is not claimed as MIT-licensed.
