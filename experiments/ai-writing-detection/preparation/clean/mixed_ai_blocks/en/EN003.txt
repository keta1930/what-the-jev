“The Preacher lamented, ‘Of making books there is no end,’ yet he failed to recognize how much he was celebrating the craft of writing. Indeed, the creation of books, experiments, journeys, and the pursuit of wealth appears to be an endless task. One problem leads to another, and despite our relentless studies, we never feel fully educated. No statue has ever been sculpted that truly reflects our aspirations. When we uncover a new continent or traverse a mountain range, we are merely greeted by another ocean or plain beyond it. In this boundless universe, there exists ample space for our most vigorous efforts and even more. Unlike the definitive works of Carlyle, which one can read to completion, nature—whether in a secluded park or near a tiny village—continually shifts with the seasons, ensuring that even a lifetime spent wandering yields fresh surprises and joys.

On this planet, there exists but one attainable wish: death. Due to various factors, we have no one to inform us whether this is a goal worth pursuing.

As we journey toward our elusive dreams, we paint a peculiar picture of ourselves, relentlessly advancing while begrudging every moment we spend resting; we are tireless, adventurous explorers. It is true that we may never arrive at our destination; in fact, it is likely that no such destination exists. Even if we were to live for centuries and wield divine abilities, we would find ourselves scarcely closer to our desires at the end. Oh, the diligent hands of humanity! Oh, the tireless feet, wandering toward an unknown future! It seems you are on the brink of reaching a notable peak, where just a little further, against the backdrop of the setting sun, you might glimpse the towers of El Dorado. Little do you realize the fortune you possess; for to journey with hope is far superior to mere arrival, and the essence of success lies in the act of striving.”
