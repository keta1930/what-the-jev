I glance around the carriage, my gaze settling on my bag. Have I packed everything? Naturally, I have. Yet, an unsettling sensation lingers, suggesting I’ve forgotten something vital. I can quickly resolve this concern. Let’s consider tonight. I’m certain my evening attire is included. Shirts, collars…

I mentally run through the entire weekend itinerary, assigning appropriate outfits for each event. Yes, it appears I’ve brought along everything I could possibly need. But what an absolutely delightful schedule I’m crafting for myself! Will it truly be as enjoyable as I envision? It certainly was during my previous visits, which is why I’m feeling so content.

The train halts at its sole stop, bathed in the warm light of a mid-September afternoon. A pleasant flurry surrounds us; friendly faces disembark, and welcoming figures greet them, creating an atmosphere of cheerfulness and satisfaction. And then, we’re off again… with the next station being mine.

We arrive. A porter greets me with a warm smile and a cheerful, “Nice day.” I spot Brant outside with the wagonette instead of the trap, indicating I’m not the only guest arriving on this train. I wonder who else is on board… Ah, it’s Bob and Mrs. Bob, and—look!—Cynthia! And isn’t that old Anderby? How wonderful! I must remember to reclaim that shilling I lost to Bob at billiards during my last visit. And if Cynthia genuinely believes she can excel at croquet…

We exchange joyful greetings and climb into the wagonette. The countryside has never appeared more beautiful. “No, not a drop of rain,” Brant reassures us, “and the barometer is rising.” The porter loads our bags into the cart, approaching us with a friendly demeanor. It’s a tough job being a porter, and I sincerely hope everyone relishes this afternoon. Plus, I find myself short on change.

I place half a crown into his hand. Now, we are all exceedingly happy.
