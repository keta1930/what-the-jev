The first tale is now behind us. The second narrative unfolds as a charming "i'-faith-and-stap-me" yarn set in a bygone era. Although it isn't particularly seasonal—most of its events transpiring in my lord's garden, filled with the fragrance of roses—it evokes those nostalgic days when conflicts and exclamations had a certain flair, and women engaged in needlework with passion. This type of narrative is best enjoyed by the warmth of a Christmas fire; it hearkens back to a time long past, fitting seamlessly into a Christmas collection. I won’t delve into the plot, as it holds little significance; what truly matters are the delightful "stap me’s" and "la, sirs." However, I can reveal that she ultimately weds him, and he departs contentedly to join the fray.

Now, we seek another story. What direction should this one take? Possibilities abound: perhaps it could feature an amateur burglar, a child who mends the rift between old Sir John and his daughter's suitor, the spectral presence at Enderby Grange, a millionaire's festive feast, or an incident involving the Scotch express. Personally, I am not particularly drawn to any of these ideas; I cast my vote for the tale of a deserted isle. In this story, the proud Lady Julia tumbles from the deck of a liner, and Ronald, spurned by her that very morning, leaps from the hurricane deck—or possibly the bowsprit—to rescue her as she sinks for the third time. It’s a fog-laden night, and their absence goes unnoticed. When dawn breaks, they find themselves stranded together on a small coral reef. Fortunately, they face no peril, as several liners are expected to pass within a day or two, and Ronald's pockets are stocked with biscuits and chocolate. However, the situation is rather uncomfortable for Lady Julia, who had wished to avoid further encounters with Ronald. They sit back to back on the beach, exchanging sarcastic banter over their shoulders. In the end, he manages to soften her haughty demeanor—I suspect by concealing the turtles' eggs from her—and the next liner to arrive carries the joyful couple back to the comforts of civilization.
