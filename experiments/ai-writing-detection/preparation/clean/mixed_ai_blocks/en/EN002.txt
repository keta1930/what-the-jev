"His understanding of why it had been taken from him eluded him completely. He and his wife had consistently exerted their utmost efforts; guests had expressed satisfaction and returned frequently, yet the luster had faded. Was his establishment not well-maintained? The linens were plush, and the service was attentive. As I observed, it was evident that neither he nor his spouse ever took a moment’s rest. They were hands-on, overseeing every detail. They spared no expense to ensure the comfort of their guests. While some innkeepers might skimp on costs, he was not one of them. He recognized that while frugality might be prudent elsewhere, it was not in his dining room. Were the meals not plentiful and varied? Could anyone find a more generous breakfast for 4 lire or a superior lunch for 5? And were they not served promptly? Was his wine not of good quality and reasonably priced?

Nevertheless, four years earlier, and for reasons unknown, the star had mysteriously vanished from his hotel. It felt unjust, a real affront. Four years ago—without any forewarning or explanation.

When he stumbled upon the latest edition of Baedeker, left behind by a guest, he could hardly believe what he saw. He immediately summoned his wife and others; they too were stunned. It struck them like a bolt from the blue, a seismic event. After all their tireless efforts, their eagerness to satisfy, and the loyalty of their regular clientele, who returned time and again, how could this be? Surely, that was the ultimate measure of success—that they kept coming back. Clearly, the guidebook had erred, perpetrating an egregious injustice.

What could have led to this situation? He theorized that perhaps one of Herr Baedeker's representatives had visited incognito and encountered an unfortunate incident—something unavoidable in the kitchen that had soured the experience. But that seemed so unjust! Judging based on a single misstep was unfair. There were still numerous competitors who retained their stars while he had lost his!

Thus, the Golden Eagle would lament daily throughout my stay, always concluding with a plea that I would assist him in regaining his star, given my supposed influence.

Now, it seems a new era of guidebook evaluation may be on the horizon. If the Golden Eagle has weathered the War, he might be more accepting of his situation in the wake of Baedeker's decline: perhaps a new guide will bestow upon him the coveted star he seeks. However, I do not intend to remain with him just to verify that possibility."
