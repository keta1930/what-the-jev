Who are these cherished spirits? It is essential to steer clear of those who engage in deep, philosophical debates. I prefer not to have thinkers like Plato, Gibbon, or any of their scholarly peers lingering at my bedside, nor do I wish to entertain poets, novelists, dramatists, or even professional comedians. While these individuals are undoubtedly remarkable in their respective fields, they belong downstairs, away from my personal space. Instead, I welcome the warm-hearted companions who approach in their cozy attire, figuratively speaking, and converse with you as if you’ve been acquainted since childhood, alongside your ancestors. Among these, old Montaigne stands out. What an exquisite conversationalist he is! He shares the most fascinating tales with an admirable openness, spilling forth his thoughts as casually as a child empties his pockets, granting you access to his entire intellectual domain. You can explore freely without encountering any signs prohibiting entry or warnings about trespassing.

And then there’s Boswell. Who could possibly imagine their bedside without the presence of dear, chatty Boswell, the finest of admirers and the most extraordinary chronicler? When Boswell begins to converse and the old Doctor interjects with a "Sir," the burdens and concerns of everyday life seem to dissipate effortlessly, and the gloomy weight of Dismal Jemmy disappears like a fleeting specter at dawn. In those moments, I am no longer confined by the constraints of time or the physical realm; instead, I find myself in the esteemed company of the eternal. I partake in their victorious detachment from the drama unfolding on our earthly stage, gaining insight into its role within the grand, ongoing narrative of humanity.
